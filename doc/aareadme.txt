I finally found some documentation that matches the version of JSON Schema that I am using. 

Quicktype uses Draft 7.  Classic JSON Schema.  I am using Quicktype. 

But the JSON Schema organization has moved on to 2020-12.  Modern JSON Schema.  All their 
documentation refers to the latest version. 

Their intro didn't cover everything. 

Their "tour" covered most things, but it talked in terms of Modern JSON Schema.  I was 
constantly going back to Gai and asking for clarification on whether a feature was 
available in Classic, and whether it took a different form in Classic. 

We had many interest and illuminating conversations about how the language evolved, 
and why, and what conversations the design committee had.  And it was my fault, 
I asked for those conversations.  But it took a lot of time. 

After a couple of days (!) I found a pdf from Michael Droettboom et al. that 
covers Classic well.  In not too many pages, too.
